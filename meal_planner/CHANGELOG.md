# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - Recipe Card Redesign

### 🌟 Features
* **A new look made for your phone.** Meals now looks like a recipe card: cream card stock on a warm paper background, tomato red for the main buttons, herb green for things that are done, and food photos up front. It follows your phone's light or dark setting (charcoal in dark mode), and every button is big enough for a thumb.
* **One day at a time.** The Plan tab shows a single day, with a strip of the next seven days across the top. Dots under each day show what's planned and what's cooked. Tap a day or swipe left and right to move between days, and use the arrows to see other weeks. Dinner gets a big photo card; breakfast and lunch sit above it.
* **Cook mode.** Tap Cook on a meal and the recipe fills the screen in large type, one step at a time, and keeps the screen on while you cook (where your phone allows it; the header says so when it's on). Tick off ingredients as you get them out, swipe or tap Next between steps, and tap any time in a step ("simmer 10 minutes") to start a timer. Timers beep when they're done (and buzz on Android), and finishing marks the meal cooked and takes the ingredients out of the pantry.
* **Leftovers.** "Cook extra for leftovers" on any meal turns another meal (tomorrow's lunch by default) into leftovers of it. The shopping list buys enough for both, the recipe scales up to match, and eating the leftovers doesn't take anything more out of the pantry. An empty meal also has a Leftovers button to pick a meal to cook bigger.
* **Move and swap meals.** Move a meal to another day or meal; whatever was there swaps into its place.
* **Recipe cards.** Tap a meal or recipe to open its card: photo, a servings stepper that rescales every amount, ingredients you still need to buy marked "to buy", the method, a 1 to 5 star rating and your own notes, which save as you type.
* **Shopping mode.** The Shop tab has a tab for each store (Costco, Costco Business Center, King Soopers) plus All, with a progress bar. Tap anywhere on an item to put it in the cart; it drops into an "In the cart" section at the bottom.
* **Works in the store with no signal.** Ticks and removals happen on your phone straight away and are sent when the signal comes back. A small "Offline" tag shows how many changes are waiting. The list is kept on your phone too, so coming back to the Shop tab works without signal while the planner is open.
* **Swipe to remove or put away.** Swipe an item left to remove it or right to put it in the pantry, with Undo for a few seconds.
* **Edit list items.** Tap the pencil on an item to change its name, amount, store or aisle. The planner remembers where you file things, so next time that item lands in the same store and aisle.
* **Smarter add box.** Type "2 lb chicken thighs", "a dozen eggs" or "milk x2" and the amount and unit are split out for you. It suggests things you've bought before, and when you're on a store's tab the item goes to that store.
* **Finish the trip.** When you're done shopping, Finish the trip lets you choose which ticked items go in the pantry and clears them off the list.
* **Pantry overhaul.** Search the pantry, items are grouped by shelf, a "Use soon" strip shows what expires first, and + and − change amounts straight away. Tap an item to edit its name, amount, shelf and best-before date with a real date picker (plus quick 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks and 1 month buttons). Items you run out of move to a "Ran out" list with a Restock button instead of disappearing.

### 🔧 Improvements
* Pop-up boxes are gone: swapping a meal, choosing a day, setting how many people are eating and confirming a removal all happen in sheets that slide up from the bottom, with idea buttons like "Something quick" or "Use what's in the pantry" when you ask Claude for a swap.
* Planning the week, connecting Claude and rebuilding the list now live behind the sparkle button on the Plan tab.
* The Shop tab icon shows how many items are left to get.
* Small amounts read naturally: 1/16 cup shows as 1 tbsp, and fractions show as ½, ⅓ and ¼.
* New icon and logo to match the recipe-card look.
